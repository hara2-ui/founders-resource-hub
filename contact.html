Frequently Asked Questions

Is this site affiliated with Y Combinator?

No. Founder's Resource Hub is an independent project. We reference YC resources because they are among the best freely available educational content for founders.

Can I suggest a tool or resource?

Absolutely. Use the form and select "Resource Suggestion" as your topic. We review all submissions and add high-quality resources regularly.

Is the newsletter free?

Yes, completely free. We will never charge for the newsletter and will never share your email address with third parties.

How often is the newsletter sent?

We aim for a bi-weekly cadence — enough to stay useful without filling your inbox. You can unsubscribe at any time.
