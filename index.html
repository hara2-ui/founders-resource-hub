Welcome

Built for founders, by a founder.

The startup journey can feel overwhelming, especially at the beginning. There is no shortage of advice on the internet — but most of it is scattered, inconsistent, and buried behind algorithms designed to keep you scrolling rather than building.

The Founder's Resource Hub was created to solve exactly that problem. This site brings together the most essential tools, frameworks, and mental models that early-stage entrepreneurs need — curated, organized, and presented in a way that respects your time and intelligence.

Whether you are still validating your idea, just shipped your MVP, or are somewhere in between, you will find something here that moves you forward. Explore the pages, bookmark what resonates, and come back often.
